🕷️ Fabi Spider-Man: Avengers Challenge 🛡️

Second Grade Reading + Quantitative Test

The Story

Fabi was Spider-Man’s young partner. Peter Parker had been training him for almost one year. Every day after school, Fabi practiced swinging, reading clues, solving problems, and helping people.

One afternoon, Peter received a message from Tony Stark.

“Bring Fabi to Avengers Tower tomorrow,” Tony wrote. “I want to see if he is ready.”

Fabi was excited. “Does this mean I can become an Avenger?”

Peter smiled. “Maybe. But being an Avenger is not only about powers. It is about courage, honesty, kindness, teamwork, and making smart choices.”

The next morning, Peter and Fabi arrived at Avengers Tower. Captain America opened the door. Tony Stark was waiting inside with fifteen special challenges.

First, Tony showed Fabi four backpacks: red, blue, green, and yellow. “The emergency kit is inside the blue backpack,” Tony said. Fabi picked the blue backpack.

Next, a little boy dropped all his books. At the same time, Tony’s robot asked Fabi to keep testing. Fabi stopped and helped the boy pick up the books.

Then Peter threw three practice drones into the air. Fabi caught two, but one flew away.

Captain America asked, “Can one hero save everyone?” Fabi answered, “Sometimes, but a team can do much more.”

Later, Tony dropped one of his tools. Nobody saw it except Fabi. Fabi picked it up and gave it back to Tony.

At the end of the day, Tony said, “You did well, Fabi. But you still have one last test: never stop learning.”

Peter smiled proudly. Fabi looked at the Avengers and said, “I’ll keep practicing.”

20. Black Widow trains for 4 days. Each day she completes 6 missions. How many missions total?

21. Thor has 18 practice hammers. He removes 9. How many are left?

23. Choose the correct symbol: 45 ___ 45
