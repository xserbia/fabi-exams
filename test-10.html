Fabi and the DeLorean

Fabi was alone in his house on Saturday morning. His parents had gone to the grocery store. Fabi was reading a comic book in the living room.

VROOOOOM!

The sound of a car caught Fabi's attention. He looked through the window and saw a shiny silver car in front of his house. The doors opened upward.

Marty McFly and Doc Brown stepped out of the car.

“Great Scott!” Doc shouted. “We found Fabi's house!”

Fabi ran outside. He could not believe his eyes.

“Is that the DeLorean?” Fabi asked.

“Yes,” Marty answered. “It is a time machine.”

Fabi told Marty and Doc about a boy named Jake. Jake often bullied Fabi at school. Fabi wanted to travel to the past and stop Jake from becoming a bully.

Doc warned Fabi that changing the past could be dangerous. Instead, they decided to visit the past to learn more about Jake.

Fabi, Marty, and Doc climbed into the DeLorean. Doc set the time circuits for five years in the past.

The car moved faster and faster. When it reached 88 miles per hour, bright blue lights surrounded it.

WHOOSH!

They arrived five years earlier. They went to Jake's school and watched him from far away.

Jake was smaller than the other children. Some older students laughed at him and pushed him. Jake looked sad and lonely.

Fabi was surprised. He understood that Jake had learned the wrong behavior from the children who bullied him.

“We cannot erase Jake's past,” Doc said, “but we can choose what to do in the present.”

They returned to Fabi's time.

On Monday, Fabi spoke kindly to Jake. He invited Jake to play basketball during recess.

Jake looked surprised. Then he smiled.

“I am sorry for being mean,” Jake said.

From that day forward, Jake stopped bullying Fabi and the other students. Fabi learned that kindness can change the future better than changing the past.
