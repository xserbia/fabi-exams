Story: Spider-Man and Fabian Save the City

Fabian was a regular second-grade boy who loved science, reading books, and helping others. One afternoon, while walking home from school, he met Spider-Man.

"Hi, Fabian," said Spider-Man. "I need a partner today."

Fabian smiled. "Really? Me?"

"Yes! Being a hero is not only about being strong. It is about making good choices."

Spider-Man gave Fabian a special red and blue suit. When Fabian put it on, he became Spider-Fab.

Their first mission was to rescue a little puppy stuck in a tree. Spider-Man climbed the tree while Fabian calmed the scared puppy.

Next, they helped an old woman carry heavy grocery bags across the street. After that, they stopped two robbers by using webs to trap them without hurting anyone.

Later, they saw smoke coming from a building. The firefighters were already helping inside, so Spider-Man and Fabian directed people safely away from the building.

When the day was over, Spider-Man smiled and said, "A real hero helps people, stays calm, works as a team, and always does the right thing."

Fabian smiled. "I can't wait for our next adventure."
