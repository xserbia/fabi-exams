Beginning 2nd Grade Practice Test

Reading Comprehension

Max's New Puppy

Max wanted a puppy for a long time.

One Saturday morning, his mom took him to the animal shelter. Max saw a small brown puppy with floppy ears. The puppy wagged its tail and licked Max's hand.

"I want this one!" Max said.

They took the puppy home and named him Buddy.

Every day, Max gave Buddy food and water. They played with a ball in the yard. At night, Buddy slept in a soft bed next to Max's room.

Max smiled because Buddy became his best friend.

1. What did Max want?

2. Where did Max go with his mom?

3. What color was the puppy?

4. What did the puppy do when it saw Max?

5. What name did Max give the puppy?

6. What did Max give Buddy every day?

7. Where did Max and Buddy play?

8. Where did Buddy sleep?

9. How did Max feel at the end?

10. What is the main idea of the story?
