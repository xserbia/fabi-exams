The Story

Fabian was home alone on a rainy Saturday afternoon. His parents had gone to the grocery store and told him they would return before dinner. Fabian finished his homework, fed his dog, Rocket, and began building a model spaceship.

At 5:15 p.m., Fabian heard a car stop outside. A moment later, the front door opened. His mother and father walked inside, but something seemed strange. They moved slowly and looked around the house as if they had never seen it before.

“Hello, young human,” said the person who looked like Fabian’s father.

Fabian raised an eyebrow. His father always called him “Champ.” Then the person who looked like his mother called Rocket “the small furry animal.” Fabian knew that his mother always called the dog by his name.

Fabian decided to test them. “What kind of cake did I have on my last birthday?” he asked.

The two visitors looked at each other. “Green vegetable cake,” they answered.

Fabian knew they were wrong. He had eaten chocolate cake with strawberries. He quietly walked upstairs and hid behind the hallway wall. From there, he watched the visitors enter the living room.

Suddenly, they removed their human masks. Underneath were two tall purple aliens with silver eyes and three fingers on each hand. One alien opened a small communication device and said, “The house is secure. Prepare the spaceship.”

Fabian stayed calm. During a school science fair, he had met Agent J from the Men in Black. Agent J had secretly given him an emergency number in case he ever saw something unusual.

Fabian went into his bedroom, locked the door, and called the number. He explained the clues: the strange greeting, the dog’s wrong name, the incorrect birthday cake, and the hidden masks.

Ten minutes later, a black car stopped in front of the house. Agent J and Agent K stepped out wearing black suits and dark sunglasses. They entered through the back door so the aliens would not see them.

Agent K gave Fabian a small silver device called a Portal Blaster. “This device will not hurt them,” she explained. “It will open a portal and send them back to their own planet.”

The agents made a plan. Agent J would distract the aliens. Agent K would protect the front door. Fabian would stand near the hallway and activate the Portal Blaster only when both aliens were close together.

Agent J walked into the living room and said, “Excuse me. Is this the meeting for visitors from another planet?”

The aliens jumped up and chased him toward the hallway. Fabian waited until both aliens were standing inside the blue target circle on the floor. Then he pressed the button.

FLASH!

A bright blue portal opened. The aliens were pulled safely into it and sent back to their planet. The portal closed, and the house became quiet again.

At 5:45 p.m., Fabian’s real parents arrived. Their car had gotten a flat tire, which had delayed them. They were surprised to see two Men in Black agents in the kitchen.

Agent J smiled at Fabian. “You noticed the clues, stayed calm, and followed the plan. Those are the skills of a great agent.”

Before leaving, Agent K handed Fabian a black card with a silver letter M. “Keep practicing,” she said. “We may need your help again.”
