Mission 1: The Lost Backpack Reading
Spider-Man was swinging through the city when he saw a red backpack sitting alone on a park bench. He looked around, but nobody was nearby. Inside the backpack, he found a lunchbox, a library book, and a student ID that said Emma. At the same time, Spider-Man heard police sirens. A small robbery was happening a few blocks away. Spider-Man had to make a quick decision. He gave the backpack to a nearby police officer and rushed away to help. Later that afternoon, Emma came to the police station. When the officer returned the backpack, Emma smiled and said, “Thank you! I thought I had lost it forever.”
Hero Clue: Good heroes are brave, but they are also responsible.

5. Which word best describes Spider-Man in this story?

Mission 2: The School Garden Reading
One morning, Spider-Man visited a school garden. The students had planted flowers and vegetables. Each day, they gave the plants a little water and made sure they had sunlight. A student named Ben wanted to help even more. He poured five buckets of water onto one tiny plant. Soon, the soil became too wet. Spider-Man smiled and said, “Plants need water, sunlight, and care. But too much of one thing can hurt them.” Ben nodded. The next day, he used only a small cup of water. A week later, the little plant had new green leaves.
Hero Clue: Helping is great, but smart helping means understanding what is needed.
